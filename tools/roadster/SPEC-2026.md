# Measured spec for the 2026 roadster, scaled to the game's 4.30 m length

These numbers come from research on the reveal coverage (Top Gear walkaround figures, plus pixel measurements of
near-orthographic side and overhead press renders, cross-checked against reported tyre sizes). The real car is
about 4.36 m long, so everything here is scaled by 0.986. Where this file and DESIGN.md disagree on numbers, this
file wins. DESIGN.md's description of the main reference photo still defines the look.

## Frame and proportions (model frame: nose +Z, z = 2.15 − f·4.30 for fraction f of length from the nose)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Length / width / height | 4.30 / 1.92 (widest at rear haunch; front fenders 1.89) / 1.14 |
| Wheelbase | 2.79; **front axle z = +1.34**, **rear axle z = −1.45** |
| Overhangs | front 0.81, rear 0.70 |
| Front tyre | 295/30 R20 → OD 0.676, width 0.29; hub height 0.338 |
| Rear tyre | 335/30 R21 → OD 0.724, width 0.33; hub height 0.362 |
| Track (hub x) | ±0.80 front, ±0.795 rear; rear tyres flush with the haunch sides in rear view |
| Arch tops | front 0.69, rear 0.75 (tight round arches, sharp lips) |
| Ground clearance | 0.11 (chin), 0.12 (sill) |

## Profile heights (m) and stations (fraction of length from the nose)

- The nose leading edge and front light bar are at 0.41–0.46. The chin splitter bottom is at 0.11.
- The hood rises at about 19° from the nose. The hood's rear centreline is at 0.70, and the fender-top corners and
  A-pillar feet are at 0.77.
- The slope runs nearly continuously from the nose up the windshield (about 18.5°) to the roof, with only a slight
  kink at the A-pillar feet (0.215).
- The windshield glass leading edge is at **0.155**, ahead of the front axle (cab-forward). The header and start
  of the T-tops are at 0.43. The roof peak is **1.14 at 0.54**; the roof is nearly flat from 0.47 to 0.62.
- Two T-top glass panels sit either side of a narrow central spine from 0.43 to 0.63. The trapezoidal rear glass
  runs from 0.63 to **0.92**, narrowing rearward to about 0.6 m wide between flat sail panels. Dark slot vents
  sit between the sails and the glass.
- The rear deck's trailing edge and the red light bar are at **0.87**, at 1.00.
- The shoulder crease is one straight line rising from 0.61 behind the front arch to 0.87 at the tail.
- The beltline (bottom of the side glass) is at 0.76 at the A-pillar and 0.81 at the B-pillar, rising to a sharp
  rear point at **0.75 L, 1.03 m**.
- The lower edge of the white body side, which is the top of the black carbon sill, rises from 0.25 behind the
  front wheel to **0.41** ahead of the rear wheel.
- The doors run from 0.33 to a forward-leaning rear cut at 0.59 (top) and 0.56 (bottom). They have no handles.
- A slim amber wedge side marker sits behind the front arch top, at 0.52 m and 0.28 L, with a small black slot
  right behind it.
- A carbon-framed rectangular vent or flap sits at each lower rear corner behind the rear wheel.

## Plan

- The nose is rounded-square with large corner chamfers (0.25–0.3 m). The hood is a trapezoid 1.35 m wide at the
  nose and 1.0 m at the windshield. The fender facets flank it to form a V in front view.
- The windshield is **hexagonal** in plan: 1.0 m at its leading edge, flaring along the inner edges of the fender
  facets to 1.55 m at the A-pillar feet, then 1.1 m at the header. The A-pillars are thin body-coloured strips.
- Body width is nearly constant from front to rear. The rear deck narrows above the haunches (tumblehome).

## Front

- A thin white light bar runs along the nose's leading edge. It is **hood-width (about 1.35 m)**, ending at the
  inboard faces of the body-coloured corner blocks.
- Headlights are hidden pop-down units. When lit, each reads as a small bright **triangle** (0.16 × 0.06 m) at the
  inboard base of each fender facet, about 0.56 m high and ±0.67 m off the centreline.
- Below the bar is a flush black satin panel. Below that is a dark recessed opening (0.10–0.15 m tall) holding an
  **active multi-element wing**: 4–5 thin horizontal black slats. Then comes a protruding carbon chin splitter
  whose ends sweep slightly forward. A small camera sits at the top centre of the opening.

## Rear

- The **full-width red LED light bar** is 1.73 m wide and 25–30 mm tall, recessed under the sharp overhanging deck
  lip at 0.87. A second thinner red line runs just below it, and small red slivers or reflectors sit at the lower
  rear corners.
- The rear fascia is body-coloured with a centred recessed plate reading **"RDSTR"** (0.30 × 0.15) and a small
  camera above it.
- Below that is an exposed **carbon diffuser "box" 1.23 m wide** with chamfered upper corners. Its **upper row holds
  the 4 round thruster nozzles in a line: 0.08 m outer diameter on 0.245 m centres (x = ±0.1225, ±0.3675)**, with
  polished titanium rims and gold/bronze throats. Below are **4 bays separated by vertical carbon fins**, each
  holding a round suction-fan outlet with a cone hub. Carbon side pieces wrap the lower rear corners.
- The active rear wing stows flush in the deck. Model it stowed: a polished bronze/titanium flush blade outline.

## Wheels

- Thin-spoke aero wheels with **6 straight radial spokes**. Each spoke is a hollow open frame: a polished
  titanium-silver outline, dark interior and a bronze inner vane, so from a distance each spoke reads as a dark
  blade with bright edges.
- Flat **white (body-colour) aero disc inserts** fill the gaps between the spokes. The centre hub is dark titanium
  with a plain cap and no logo.
- Tyres are low profile (30 series) with plain black sidewalls.

## Materials

- Body: "Ano White", a satin, anodised-looking warm off-white (#E9E6E0), roughness 0.35–0.45, low clearcoat.
- Lower body, sills and diffuser: black and carbon fibre.
- Glass: gold/bronze tint (#6B4A2F) with strong metallic reflection; from outside it reads dark bronze with gold
  reflections.
- Interior: ultra-white seats with grey bolsters, a yoke, a centre screen and titanium accents. Through the glass
  the cabin reads warm brown/copper.
